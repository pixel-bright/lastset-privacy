Privacy Policy

Last updated 18 October 2026

The short version: Last Set does not collect, transmit, or sell any of your data. Everything you log stays on your phone. There are no accounts, no servers, and no tracking.

Who this covers

This policy applies to the Last Set mobile app ("the app"), published by Pixel Bright ("we", "us"). It explains how the app handles your information — which, in short, is by leaving it entirely on your own device.

What we collect

Nothing. Every workout, exercise, set, rep, and weight you enter is stored only in a database on your device. The app has no user accounts and no backend servers, so your training data never leaves your phone or reaches us in any form.

What we share

Nothing. We cannot share, sell, or disclose data that we never collect or receive. No data is passed to advertisers, data brokers, or any third party.

Analytics and tracking

The app contains no analytics, no advertising, and no third-party tracking software. We do not measure how you use the app, and nothing reports your activity back to us or anyone else.

Exporting and sharing your own data

If you choose to export or back up your data, or share it from the app, that action is initiated by you and the file goes only where you direct it — to a location or app you pick. The app does not send these files to us or to any automatic destination.

Your control and deletion

Because all of your data lives on your device, you are fully in control of it. Uninstalling the app, or clearing its storage in your device settings, permanently and completely removes your data. We hold no copy, so there is nothing for us to access, recover, or delete on your behalf.

Children

Last Set is intended for people aged 18 and over and is not directed at children.

Changes to this policy

If this policy changes, the updated version will be posted on this page with a new "last updated" date. Because the app collects no data, any changes are likely to be minor clarifications.
